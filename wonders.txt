The Great Wall of China is a series of fortifications built across the historical northern borders of ancient Chinese states.
Petra is a historical and archaeological city in southern Jordan, famous for its rock-cut architecture.
The Colosseum is an elliptical amphitheatre in the centre of the city of Rome, Italy, built of travertine limestone, tuff, and brick-faced concrete.
Chichén Itzá was a large pre-Columbian city built by the Maya people of the Terminal Classic period, located in Mexico.
Machu Picchu is a 15th-century Inca citadel located in the Eastern Cordillera of southern Peru on a mountain ridge.
The Taj Mahal is an ivory-white marble mausoleum on the right bank of the river Yamuna in Agra, India.
Christ the Redeemer is an Art Deco statue of Jesus Christ in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, created by French sculptor Paul Landowski.
